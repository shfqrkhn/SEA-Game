## Recovery brochure readback

On 2026-10-09 the two-page [HX10x10 recovery brochure](https://www.rheinmetall.com/Rheinmetall%20Group/brochure-download/Vehicle-Systems/D1140e0724-HX-10x10-special-recovery-vehicle.pdf) was read directly. Page 1 specifies length 11.6 m, width 2.5 m, height 3.5 m and up to three cabin occupants. Page 2 identifies a main capstan winch, two rotator winches and an integrated underlift. These dimensions, ten-wheel topology, capacity and equipment ratings do **not** describe SEA's synthetic eight-wheel Recovery vehicle. They cannot validate its current approximately 6.25 m model or supply its game ratings. Use this source only to constrain connected recovery-system packaging; dimensioned acceptance of the original SEA design remains open. No brochure imagery, brand marks or manufacturer asset files are embedded.

Further required reference work: dimensioned seat/restraint systems, vehicle suspension and steering/brake packaging, clearance equipment, protection panel cross-sections, interior/hatch clearances and variant-specific service routing. These are open reference gaps, not completed research.
