# Three.js game qualification

Both role applications now use a primary 3D workshop across all eight game phases. Six mission vehicles, 70 canonical purchased-card models and one training model share olive PBR paint, metal, rubber/glass and physical scale. Process cards are engineering review desks; titles, effects, scores and rules remain in the authoritative bilingual HTML.

The public projection copies only phase, role, language, mission/team identifiers, revealed current-card title/ID and selected-team owned title/IDs. Hidden market order, seed, private plans/notes and submission drafts never enter the 3D module. The configuration displays the latest purchase per category on separate stations; every owned card remains inspectable and recorded in the ledger. Models are illustrative concepts, not manufactured-vehicle specifications or certified mechanical integrations.

One WebGL context per application, pixel-ratio cap 1.5, on-demand frames, no auto-motion, resize/camera fitting, accessible dropdowns, preset-view buttons, disposal and context-error status bound resource use. Pinned Three.js 0.186.1 and OrbitControls are bundled locally under MIT. All runtime code, geometry/materials and print illustrations are embedded in each HTML. No runtime CDN/fetch, external artwork-loading chain or alternative 2D selector is used. CSP denies image/connection loading. A checked-in receipt binds bundle bytes to the exact geometry/renderer inputs and authoring lockfile. The existing hash-bound CSP and schema-3 save format are preserved.

[Model verification](evidence/convergence/threejs-game-20261008/model-verification.json) checks crew-seat counts against canonical capacity and all mesh vertices in 12 camera/aspect combinations for each of 77 models. Pure adapter tests cover manual hidden/revealed, JIT/round visibility, role isolation, copying and every lifecycle phase. Existing production command/journey tests cover rules, correction, monetary boundaries and recovery; those VM tests do not prove browser rendering.

[Live preview](https://shfqrkhn.github.io/SEA-Game/previews/threejs-game-20261008/) is an additive publication separate from the existing root release. Built-in browser and exact remote-byte evidence are recorded in the adjacent evidence folder. Classroom/content judgment, low-powered device performance, assistive technology, and final release/operations acceptance remain open. No 100% or full three-pass convergence claim is made.

[Built-in-browser journey](evidence/convergence/threejs-game-20261008/browser-journey.json) records the live 70-lot auction, purchase recovery, privacy gate, debrief and closure. This journey spans dated preview revisions and is scoped evidence, not a fresh final-candidate full run. Crew capacity was subsequently corrected through an observed CAP-C 6-versus-4 assertion and passing geometry check.
