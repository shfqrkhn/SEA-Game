# Art review (owner, about 5 minutes)

**Why:** realism is your judgement (MPES §1.4, D-14). The agent has already checked the renders for clipping, floating parts, framing and exposure.

**You need:** this PC or any Windows 11 laptop, with Edge or Chrome.

1. Open the QA build: `https://shfqrkhn.github.io/SEA-Game/`, or `dist/index.html` from the handoff folder.
2. On the first screen, look at the hero vehicle. Ask yourself: does it look like a real armoured vehicle photographed in a studio?
3. Click **Student team**, join with any code the instructor screen gives you (or follow the quick-start guide), and choose any mission.
4. Watch the vehicle turn for about 10 seconds. Drag it with the mouse, and press **Pause**.
5. Record two or three purchases. Each part should appear on the vehicle, sitting on it, not floating or poking through.
6. Optional: open `.artifacts/contact/cards.jpg` (all 71 cards) and `.artifacts/contact/builds-A.jpg` (each vehicle with a full set of parts).

**Reply with one line:**

- `GO`, or
- `FIX: <what looks wrong>`, for example "FIX: tyres too shiny", or "FIX: the combat turret looks like a toy".

Rough notes are fine. The agent turns them into changes and asks for nothing else.
